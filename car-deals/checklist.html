Why a "🔥 Hot" deal can still be bad: the price-vs-(year+km) regression doesn't see accident history, service records, owner count, trim, or rental/fleet use. If a 2-3 year old car has 100k+ km, assume fleet/rideshare unless service history proves otherwise. Use this checklist before handing over money.

Toyota hybrid warranty (Canada): hybrid components covered 10 years / 240,000 km from in-service date (or 8 yr / 160,000 on older models). Verify in-service date — it's the warranty start, not model year.

6. Interior & electronics

7. Test drive (≥20 min, mixed)

8. Independent inspection (highly recommended)

Best $150 you'll spend. Insist on a pre-purchase inspection (PPI) at an independent shop or the brand's dealer, on a hoist. Walk away if the seller refuses — they're hiding something.
