RAV4 LE — Viewing Checklist

2023 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid LE AWD · VIN 2T3BWRFVXPW156292 · ~53,200 km · White
OpenRoad Toyota Richmond · 13100 Smallwood Place, Richmond, BC · (604) 273-3768
Already covered by the dealer's 210-point inspection (May 5): mechanicals, hybrid battery (green), brakes (machined + serviced), alignment (corrected), tires (8/32" all 4), all electronics. Your job today: the body / Oct 2023 incident, test drive feel, and the paperwork.

1 · Before you go 2 min

  • Confirm by text: TCUV certification being added + appointment time
  • Bring: photo ID, proof of insurance, financing details if applicable. No money changes hands today.
  • Have phone fully charged (CarPlay/Android Auto test)

2 · Body walk-around — daylight 10 min

  • VIN matches: dashboard plate + driver doorjamb sticker = 2T3BWRFVXPW156292
  • Panel gaps even all the way around (hood, doors, trunk). Run a finger along — any sudden steps = bodywork
  • Paint match in DAYLIGHT — adjacent panels identical shade. Look from low angle. Different sheens = repaint
  • Rear bumper — most common rear-end zone. Look for overspray on edges, mismatched texture
  • Front bumper / corners — same check
  • Wheel arches — overspray on inside of fenders = repaint
  • Rubber door seals + glass edges — any colour speckling = paint overspray
  • Hood + trunk open/close cleanly, hinges align, latches feel firm
  • All exterior lights: high/low beam, fog, brake (need helper), turn, reverse, plate
  • Tires: same brand all 4, no sidewall damage, DOT date code <6 yr old
  • Glass: no chips on windshield, all rear glass clear
  • Underbody: quick look for fresh leaks, exhaust intact, no rust on subframe
🚩 If you see ANY signs of past repair → ask salesperson directly: "What's this from?" Their reaction tells you everything.

3 · Interior 5 min

  • Mileage on dash ≈ 53,200 (matches paperwork)
  • Wear consistent with mileage — steering wheel, driver seat bolster, pedal pads = light wear only
  • All warning lights go OFF after engine start
  • AC blows cold, heat blows hot, defroster works front + rear
  • All windows up/down from each switch
  • All door locks from each door + key fob (test both fobs)
  • Heated seats / steering (if equipped)
  • Infotainment: pair phone via CarPlay/Android Auto, Bluetooth audio works
  • Backup camera: clear image, lines align
  • All seat adjustments work (incl. driver power if equipped)
  • Sunroof open/close/tilt (if equipped)
  • No mildew smell (water leak), no smoke, no chemical cover-up

4 · Test drive 20 min, mixed: parking lot → side streets → highway → back

  • Cold start if possible: engine turns briefly then settles. No knock, smoke, rough idle
  • EV-only at low speed: at 30 km/h, engine should stay off occasionally
  • Engine ↔ EV transitions on accel / lift / brake — smooth, no clunks
  • Steering centred at 60 km/h on straight flat road. Let go briefly — car tracks straight
  • Hard accel in safe spot — quick response, no hesitation, no warning lights
  • Hard brake from 50 km/h — straight stop, no pull, no vibration
  • Bumps + potholes — listen for clunks/knocks (heavier hybrid = suspension wears faster)
  • AWD test: find loose surface (gravel shoulder). Accel from stop. No binding, chatter, or hesitation engaging rear axle
  • Highway 100-110 km/h for 3+ min. No steering vibration (= alignment/balance OK)
  • Cruise + dynamic radar cruise: set, hold, follows traffic
  • Lane departure warning: drift to edge, gets a chime/vibration
  • Hybrid system temp gauge: engine warms normally, no overheating
  • After test drive: re-check under hood + under car for fresh leaks (oil, coolant)
  • Re-check tire pressures (TPMS not flashing)

5 · Back at the desk — BEFORE signing 5 min

  • TCUV in writing in the Comments box: "Vehicle to be Toyota Certified Used at delivery, +7 yr / 160,000 km powertrain warranty, +6 mo / 10,000 km comprehensive, at no extra cost. TCUV certificate + 160-point inspection report provided to purchaser."
  • Doc fee — pushed for $399 (was $595)? Or accepted?
  • Open Road Foundation $10 — kept or removed
  • No surprise add-ons: no paint protection, no extended (3rd-party) warranty, no nitrogen tires, no key insurance, no LoJack
  • Total purchase price matches the quote (or the negotiated number)
  • Both key fobs included
  • Floor mats included (ask)
  • Owner's manual + service booklet present
  • Delivery date confirmed

🚩 Walk-away triggers

  • Refuses to put TCUV in writing in the Comments box
  • Visible repair work on body they can't explain
  • New "fees" appear that weren't on the original quote
  • Warning lights on dash during cold start
  • Drivetrain shudder, clunks, or pulling during test drive
  • Mileage on car doesn't match the quote
  • VIN doesn't match in all locations
  • Pressure tactics, "today only" urgency, "another buyer waiting"
